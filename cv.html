Bio

Noah Scherner (b. 2004) is a Brazilian visual artist currently based in Rio de Janeiro.

Born into a lineage of architects and psychoanalysts, his practice is a continuous dialogue between the structural rigor of form and the fluid manifestations of the unconscious.

His research, characterized by "Cerrado-Futurism" and surrealist influences, operates as an act of surrender to intuition and resistance to the didactic. Scherner treats oil paint as a living partner — respecting its time, its chemical maturation, and the inherent unpredictability of the medium.

His works frequently explore the ambiguity of signs, where objects and figures exist in a state of constant transformation.
