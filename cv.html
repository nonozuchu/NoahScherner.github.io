Bio

Noah Scherner (n. 2005) e um artista visual brasileiro. Criado em Uberlandia (MG), desenvolve e consolida sua pesquisa poetica no Rio de Janeiro desde 2024.

Sua pratica concentra-se na pintura a oleo, articulando elementos do expressionismo, surrealismo contemporaneo e psicanalise. E representado pela Cana Galeria (RJ).

Sua producao atual e desenvolvida na EAV Parque Lage (RJ). Possui formacao tecnica em pintura figurativa pelo Atelier Madrid (Espanha), New Masters Academy e Joules e Joules (SP).

Noah Scherner (b. 2005) is a Brazilian visual artist. Raised in Uberlandia (MG), he has been developing and consolidating his poetic research in Rio de Janeiro since 2024.

His practice focuses on oil painting, articulating elements of expressionism, contemporary surrealism, and psychoanalysis. He is represented by Cana Galeria (RJ).

His current production is developed at EAV Parque Lage (RJ). He holds technical training in figurative painting from Atelier Madrid (Spain), New Masters Academy, and Joules e Joules (SP).
