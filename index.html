Information

Noah Scherner (b. 2004) is a Brazilian visual artist currently based in Rio de Janeiro. Born into a lineage of architects and psychoanalysts, his practice is a continuous dialogue between the structural rigor of form and the fluid manifestations of the subconscious.

His research, characterized by the "Cerrado-Futurismo" and surrealist influences, operates as an act of surrender to intuition and resistance to the didactic. Scherner treats oil paint as a living partner—respecting its time, its chemical maturation, and the inherent unpredictability of the medium. His works often explore the ambiguity of signs, where objects and figures exist in a state of constant transformation.

Currently a student at the Escola de Artes Visuais do Parque Lage, his work has been exhibited in institutions and galleries across Brazil and Spain, including recent shows at Cana Galeria. He is currently preparing for international mobility within the European Union, focusing on the technical traditions of painting and sculpture.

Rio de Janeiro, 2026.
